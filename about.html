The First India Mission

Building AI infrastructure for the Indian SME.

We believe that having a great product shouldn't require a large marketing agency. We're on a mission to democratize autonomous marketing for founders across India.

Our Story

Founders shouldn't do marketing at 11 PM.

Small and medium businesses in India struggle to scale efficiently. Hiring outreach teams and marketing assistants is expensive, and most existing platforms are designed for global enterprises with dedicated teams and dollar-based pricing.

We built Sutra to be an end-to-end marketing platform designed specifically for SMEs and new-age startups. By combining deep consumer intelligence with AI-generated creatives, we allow businesses to understand their market, launch targeted campaigns, and scale—all from one platform, without the agency bill.
