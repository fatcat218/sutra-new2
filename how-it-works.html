The Workflow

From a fuzzy idea to a live, optimizing campaign.

Instead of using six different tools to run marketing, Sutra acts as your end-to-end infrastructure. Here is exactly how it works under the hood.

01

Consumer Research

Most businesses today create ads without deeply understanding their target audience. Sutra helps businesses conduct AI-powered audience research across three key dimensions: demographic, socio-economic, and behavioural analysis.

By analyzing district-level insights, cultural nuances, and employment patterns, you identify your ideal customers before launching campaigns.

02

Ad Storming & Design

Based on the insights generated, Sutra helps you create marketing campaigns tailored to specific audience segments. Instead of generating random creatives, the system uses intelligence to produce targeted hooks.

Generate realistic actor advertisements, voice-over ads, promotional videos, and detailed second-by-second scripts for your own production team.

03

Use Case Library

Don't start from scratch. Choose from a growing library of pre-built marketing workflows tailored to your industry. Whether it's a real-estate locality story or a gaming gameplay hook, use frameworks that have historically performed well.

04

Launch & Distribute

Seamless campaign deployment through direct integrations. Manage Meta Ads, Google Display, YouTube, and TikTok from a single unified interface instead of relying on multiple disconnected tools.

05

Optimize & Scale

Testing multiple creatives manually requires time and expertise. Sutra includes an AI-assisted A/B testing layer that continuously tracks performance metrics (CPA, CTR, ROAS) and compares how different variations perform.

The system auto-shifts your budget towards the winners, ensuring lower customer acquisition costs over time.
